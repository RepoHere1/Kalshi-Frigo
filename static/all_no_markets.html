Every row below is a *NO* LANE contract entered with a resting limit order at the side's bid — not by crossing the spread, which is what the backtested strategy requires (Kalshi taker fees are 7% against 1.75% for makers, and the 84–92¢ NO band carries the favorite-longshot edge). Prices are the limit actually rested, not the ask a taker would have paid.
Separated from the lanes. This book's money, positions and closed trades are its own: a fake ${{ '%.0f'|format(book.starting) }} seeded once and never re-funded, and every row tagged strategy = all_no. The BTC / ETH / XRP / HYPE up-down lanes keep their own clips, tallies and balances and never appear here.
Sizing: full-Kelly. Clip size is f* = (fair × b − (1−fair)) / b with b = (1−price) / price for a *NO* LANE contract, multiplied by {{ '%.2f'|format(kelly.multiplier) }} and capped at {{ '%.0f'|format(kelly.max_clip_pct * 100) }}% of the book per clip, rounded to whole contracts with a floor of one. f* is the share of the book at risk, so a ${{ '%.2f'|format(kelly.example.stake * 400 if kelly.example.stake else 0) }} clip at fair 0.90 against a 88¢ limit is the worked example. Read-only — nothing here places, cancels or modifies an order.
