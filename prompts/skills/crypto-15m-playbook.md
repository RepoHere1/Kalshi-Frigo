# 15-minute crypto lane playbook (operator policy)

- The lanes enter only the 0.40-0.55 band on the favored side; do not argue
  for entries outside the operator's configured band.
- Never chase: 3 or more same-side losses in a row inside the window is a veto.
- Kill entries when a scheduled macro event (FOMC/CPI/Powell/ETF) lands inside
  the window, or when the 5-minute move exceeds 0.6%.
- Fewer, better entries beat more trades; the fee makes coin flips lose.
- Under 120 seconds to close: no new entries, period.
