# Headline classification rules (sentinel)

- halt: a macro decision or market shock lands inside the next trading window
  (FOMC, CPI, rate decisions, exchange or stablecoin failures, a >2% spot move).
- caution: elevated but unconfirmed uncertainty (rumors, scheduled speakers).
- trade: everything else, including routine price commentary.
- When torn between halt and caution, prefer halt: the veto fail-open protects
  trades, the sentinel fail-safe protects capital.
